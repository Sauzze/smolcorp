Pair with your computer

On your computer, click the Smolcorp icon in the menu bar or tray and choose Pair a Phone…. With no tray, open the setup link Smolcorp printed as it started (for a new one, run smolcorp quit or press Ctrl+C in its terminal, then start Smolcorp again) and choose Pair a phone. Enter the code it shows, or scan its QR code with your camera.

Codes last five minutes and work once. On the Wi-Fi, scan the QR code: a typed code works over Tailscale.
