# The office's recordings

Both are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). They're cut and
encoded from Freesound's previews of the originals, and credited here
though CC0 doesn't ask for it.

## keys.m4a, keys.webm

Twelve key presses, then a space bar and an enter, each isolated, evened
out and faded, in a quarter-second slot of its own.

- "Mechanical Keyboard Typing (Treble Version)" by stu556: a Leopold
  FC660M with Cherry MX Brown switches.
  https://freesound.org/people/stu556/sounds/450282/ (CC0 1.0)

## talk.m4a, talk.webm

Two five-second passages of people talking, low-passed until no word can
be made out, each in a 5.5-second slot.

- "conference chatter 3.aif" by reecord2: a conference's chatter between
  speakers, from 1:09 and 1:47.
  https://freesound.org/people/reecord2/sounds/85040/ (CC0 1.0)
