Your team

Each project's agent, your way. Anything on Auto is as the project's name picks it, and clothes on Auto follow the dress code below.

Dress code

What agents and their interns wear, by the size of the model they run: the bigger, the smarter, unless you say otherwise. The office tells a model's size from the words in its name; your own rules come first. While an agent's model isn't known, as for most agents but Claude Code, it dresses by agent. A project's own outfit on its team card comes before all of this.
